Key points

What this project covers and found, in brief.

  • A poker trainer built on an authored library of 49 scenarios, each with an answer meant to match solver play.
  • Answers are audited against a heads-up postflop solver: 28 of 49 are verified now, up from 22 of 45 at the first audit.
  • The 14 preflop spots can't be audited by that solver yet.
  • Includes the shipped GTO poker trainer and a design-system pass on the app.

Limits

  • All 14 preflop scenarios are unaudited: the heads-up postflop solver doesn't cover them
  • Eight of the agreeing answers agree only after being re-authored to match the solver
  • 34 of 34 pipeline-validation passes are a gate (invariants hold), not an accuracy figure

Outcome: Partly. Goal: every scenario's answer checked against a solver. 28 of 49 are, up from 22 of 45 at the first audit; 20 can't be audited yet (all 14 preflop spots are outside the heads-up postflop solver).
